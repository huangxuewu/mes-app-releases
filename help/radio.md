# MES Radio

A quick word can get the whole team moving.

Use Radio when you need to reach coworkers without leaving the job. Join the
channel your team uses, hold the talk control to speak, and release it when finished.
Replay received messages when you need to hear a detail again.

Allow microphone access when asked. You need your workplace's MES access and a
network connection. If you cannot hear your team, check your channel and listening
volume. Radio does not replace your workplace's emergency communication procedures.

Updates are available in MES Store. Install over the existing app to retain settings
and voice history.
