# Dispatcher

Know what's arriving before it reaches the dock.

Dispatcher is for the warehouse team planning around incoming and outgoing loads.
Use **Today** for the day's overview, **Outbounds** for departing shipments, and
**Inbounds** for arrivals. Check the relevant load before coordinating the next handoff.

Use your workplace's MES access. If a load or its status needs correcting, follow
your team's dispatch process. Contact your MES administrator if you cannot connect.

Updates are available in MES Store. Install over the existing app to retain its data.
