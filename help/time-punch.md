# Time Punch

Your time at work deserves a clear record.

Use the workplace Time Punch device to record the start of your shift, your breaks,
and the end of your shift. Enter your assigned PIN, follow the photo prompt, and
choose the appropriate work or break action. Check the result before leaving the device.

Use the available language selection if needed. If you miss a punch or choose the
wrong action, follow your workplace's correction process and contact your supervisor.
Your MES administrator manages device connection and employee access.

Updates are available in MES Store.
